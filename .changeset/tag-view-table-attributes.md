The structure keeps what a tagged table says about its cells: `summary` on a `Table`, `scope` (`Row`, `Column`, `Both`) and `id` on a `TH`, and `headers` on a `TH` or `TD` (the ids of the header cells that label it). The tags panel shows a header cell's scope, a table's summary, and the headers of the selected cell by their text; a table's "Columns" line no longer lists row headers.
