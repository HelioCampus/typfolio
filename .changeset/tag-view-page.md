The tags panel draws the page beside the tags, with the page's pdf.js, and marks the selected tag on it. Moving over the page marks the tag under the pointer and a click selects it in the list; the page follows the selection, and a "Page" button hides it. The mark covers the tag's text or a figure's own box. The panel now keeps the pdf.js document open while it is the PDF on show, and frees it when another takes its place or the panel is removed.
